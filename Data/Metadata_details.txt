Tracks individual cloud sampling events with chemical composition, fluxes, and field measurements

CLOUD_MONTH Table:

Contains 49 records with monthly aggregated cloud data
Primary keys: SITE_ID, YEAR, MONTH
Aggregates cloud measurements by month including deposition totals and cloud fraction statistics
Last updated: 4/12/2011

Both tables include measurements of various ions (H, CA, MG, K, NA, NH4, SO4, NO3, CL), deposition fluxes, and physical parameters like wind speed, liquid water content, and sample volumes.

DRYCHEM Table:

Contains 157,939 records of dry deposition measurements
Primary key: LAB_KEY
Last updated: 9/30/2025
Tracks particulate and gaseous air pollutants collected on different filter types:

Teflon filters: SO4, NO3, NH4, and cations (Ca, Mg, Na, K, Cl)
Nylon filters: SO4 and HNO3 (nitric acid)
Whatman filters: SO2 and NO3


Includes calculated totals for SO2 and NO3
Features extensive quality control flags and comment codes
Sample types: Day (D), Night (N), Composite (A, B), Continuous (C), Colocated (H)

This table complements the cloud water deposition data (CLOUD_EVENT and CLOUD_MONTH) to provide a complete picture of atmospheric deposition - both wet (cloud) and dry deposition pathways.

Fluxes reported in kg/ha
Includes % of replaced deposition velocity (Vd) values
Last updated: 9/30/2025

OZONE Table:

Contains 26,412,001 records of continuous ozone measurements
Primary keys: SITE_ID, DATE_TIME
Tracks hourly ozone concentrations in ppb
Extensive quality flags including:

Instrument issues: B, D, F, H, J (channel down, power failure, malfunction)
Data quality: I (suspect), P (potential problem), S (out of DQO)
Special cases: C (calibration), E (exceptional event), T (third party audit)
Flow/technical: K (non-nominal flow), Y (QC control points)
Value issues: < (missing <25%), _ (negative reading), ^ (alarm high >120 ppb)


QA levels 0-3, plus GS (government shutdown) and X (placeholder)
Note: Data with QA_CODE < 3 is preliminary and subject to change
Last updated: 10/2/2025
